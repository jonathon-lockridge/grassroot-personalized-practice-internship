# Sources

- Topics follow AP Physics C: Mechanics Unit 3 (Work, Energy, and Power) and Unit 4 (Linear Momentum), plus center of mass, from the College Board course page.
- Formulas and constants (g = 9.8 m/s², G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²) follow the College Board's 2025 AP Physics C: Mechanics exam reference sheet.
- Every problem, step, option, feedback message and hint was written for this project.
- 2026-11-20: every problem now opens with a "which principle fits?" step, every wrong option has a "why it's wrong" message, and five problems were added so each skill appears in at least two problems (research report, changes 3 and 4). Every number answer was recomputed by computer on 2026-11-20, and each tolerance was checked to accept sensible rounding but reject common mistakes (wrong sign, missing cos or ½, a left-out mass). Tolerances accept an answer rounded to two significant figures. The pendulum starts at 37° (cos 37° ≈ 0.80), because at 60° the common mistake L cos θ gives the same drop as the right L(1 − cos θ).
